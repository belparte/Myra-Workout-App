Your Roadmap

Plan & Progress

8-week overload + sciatica care

Weekly Schedule
Mon — Upper Push (Chest/Shoulders/Tris)
Tue — Lower Body + Core (back-safe)
Wed — Walk 20–30 min + piriformis stretch
Thu — Upper Pull (Back/Biceps)
Fri — Lower Body + Glutes
Sat — Walk + foam roll
Sun — Full Rest
Sciatica Protocol
🦵 Piriformis stretch — daily, both sides, 30s hold × 3
🧘 Cat-cow — every morning, 10 slow reps
💪 Glute bridges — every session warm-up, relieves nerve pressure
❄️ Ice after flare-up — 15 min on left glute/SI joint
🚫 Avoid — deep forward lunges, heavy deadlifts, sitting 60+ min
Progressive Overload

✅ Weeks 1–2 · Foundation (15–20 min)

Light weights, perfect form. 2 sets per exercise. Short sessions to build the habit without aggravating sciatica.

Weeks 3–4 · Build (20–30 min)

Move to 3 sets. Add 2–3 reps per exercise. Reduce rest by 10s where comfortable.

Weeks 5–6 · Load (30–35 min)

Add 2.5–5 lbs on main lifts. 3–4 sets on compounds. Full program sessions.

Weeks 7–8 · Advance (35–45 min)

Resistance bands added. Consider 4th training day. Track 1RM progress.

Every 8 Weeks · Deload

2 sets, 50% weight. Full recovery week. You'll come back stronger.

Recovery Targets
😴 7.5–9 hrs sleep — muscle is built during sleep
🥩 160–180g protein/day
💧 100+ oz water daily
🧻 Foam roll 10 min post-workout
🧘 Cat-cow + child's pose daily
