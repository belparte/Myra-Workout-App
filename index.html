Your Roadmap

Progression

8-week overload plan + recovery

Weekly Structure
Mon — Upper Push (Chest/Shoulders/Tris)
Tue — Lower Body + Core
Wed — Active Walk 30–45 min
Thu — Upper Pull (Back/Biceps)
Fri — Lower Body + Glutes
Sat — Active Walk 30–45 min
Sun — Full Rest
Progressive Overload

Weeks 1–2 · Foundation

Light weights, perfect form. Log every set. Don't rush.

Weeks 3–4 · Add Reps

Add 2–3 reps per exercise. Reduce rest by 10s where comfortable.

Weeks 5–6 · Add Weight

Add 2.5–5 lbs on main lifts. Move to 4 sets on compounds.

Weeks 7–8 · Advance

Add resistance bands. Consider a 4th training day if energy allows.

Every 8 Weeks · Deload

2 sets, 50% weight for one full week. Joints and CNS recover. You come back stronger.

Recovery Targets
😴 Sleep 7.5–9 hrs — muscle is built during sleep
🥩 160–180g protein/day — non-negotiable for recomp
💧 100+ oz water — especially on training days
🧻 Foam roll 10 min post-workout minimum
🧘 Cat-cow + child's pose daily — 3 min for your back
